circled Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

circled ("the app", "we", "us") is made by Krishna Nair. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it's kept, and the choices you have. We've tried to keep it short and plain.

The short version:

  • You don't need an account, and we never ask for your name, email address or home address.
  • Your homes, tasks and history are stored on your phone, not on our servers.
  • If you allow it, the app sends anonymous usage statistics so we can improve it. You can turn this off at any time in Settings › Share usage data.
  • We don't sell your information, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

1. Information stored on your phone

To build and keep your maintenance plan, the app stores this on your phone:

  • The homes you add: the name you give each home (for example "Main house"), its type (single family, townhouse or condo), your answer about whether an HOA looks after the outside, and the items you pick (for example "Central AC" or "Gutters").
  • Your tasks: names, how often they repeat, due dates, planned dates and times, and snoozes.
  • Your history: the dates you marked tasks done, and any notes you add.
  • Optional details: an HVAC filter size or fridge filter model, if you enter them.
  • Your settings: reminder preferences (time of day, lead time, quiet hours) and your "Share usage data" choice.

This information stays on your phone. We don't receive it and can't see it.

Phone backups. Your phone's own backup (iCloud Backup on iPhone, Google backup on Android) may include the app's data, so it can be restored to a new phone. Those backups are handled by Apple or Google under their privacy policies and your phone's settings, not by us.

2. Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your phone using its built-in notification system. No reminder information is sent to us. The app asks your permission before sending notifications. You can turn notifications off at any time, in the app (Settings › Reminders) or in your phone's settings.

3. Anonymous usage statistics

If Share usage data is on (it's on when you first install the app), the app sends information about how the app is used to our analytics provider, Mixpanel. This helps us understand, for example, whether setup is quick, whether reminders help people get tasks done, and which features are used.

What is sent:

  • What happens in the app: for example, that onboarding was completed, a reminder was scheduled or opened, a task was marked done or snoozed, a frequency was changed, or a home was added.
  • Details about those events: which built-in task it was (for example "Change HVAC filter"; tasks you create yourself are only recorded as "custom"), its priority, how early or late it was done, and similar counts and settings.
  • Basic details: your phone's platform (iPhone or Android), the app version, the type of the home you're viewing, and how many homes you track.
  • A random ID created by the app on your phone. It isn't linked to your name, email, phone number, Apple or Google account, or advertising ID. Deleting all data in the app creates a new one.

What is never sent: your name, email address, home names or addresses, task names you write yourself, notes, filter sizes, or anything else you type into the app.

No location: the app doesn't use your location, and it tells Mixpanel not to estimate one from your device's internet (IP) address.

How to turn it off: go to Settings › Share usage data and switch it off. The app then stops sending usage statistics straight away.

Mixpanel processes this data for us as a service provider. You can read Mixpanel's privacy policy at https://mixpanel.com/legal/privacy-policy.

4. What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your information.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We don't use your information to make decisions about you.

5. How long information is kept

  • On your phone: until you delete it. You can delete everything with Settings › Delete all data, and deleting the app also removes it from your phone.
  • Usage statistics: kept by Mixpanel for up to 2 years, and then deleted.

Because usage statistics are anonymous, we usually can't tell which statistics came from you. If you'd like us to try to find and delete them, email us and we'll do our best.

6. Children

circled is meant for adults who look after a home. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Turn off usage statistics: Settings › Share usage data.
  • Turn off reminders: Settings › Reminders, or your phone's settings.
  • Delete your data: Settings › Delete all data, or delete the app.
  • Questions or requests: email us at the address below. Depending on where you live (for example, California), you may have extra rights, such as asking what information we hold about you. We'll respond to any request we receive.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above. For important changes, we'll also let you know in the app.

9. Contact

Questions, requests or feedback: support@staycircled.com
