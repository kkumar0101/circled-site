?Frequently Asked Questions

My reminders aren't arriving.
  1. On your phone, check that notifications are allowed for circled: Settings › Notifications › circled on iPhone, or Settings › Apps › circled › Notifications on Android.
  2. In circled, open Settings › Reminders and check that reminders are on, and that the time of day and quiet hours are what you expect.
  3. Open circled now and then. Each time you do, it refills the reminders scheduled on your phone.
Where is my data stored?

On your phone, not online. It's included in your phone's own backup (iCloud Backup or Google backup), so restoring a new phone from a backup keeps your homes and history.

How do I start over?

Settings › Delete all data. This removes all your homes, tasks and history from the phone and takes you back to setup. It can't be undone.

Can I track more than one home?

Yes, up to 3. Tap your home's name at the top of the Home or Tasks tab, then Add a home.

A task doesn't apply to me, or I do it on a different schedule.

Open the task. You can change how often it repeats (or its months, for seasonal tasks), rename it or delete it. To add or remove optional tasks, use + Add task on the Tasks tab.

I marked something done by mistake.

Open the task and tap the Last done box or the entry in History. You can change its date or remove it, and the next due date is worked out again.

What does "Share usage data" do?

When it's on, the app sends anonymous statistics about how the app is used, never your name, home names or notes. Turn it off any time in Settings. See our Privacy Policy.
